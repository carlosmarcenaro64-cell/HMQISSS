How to Play?

Guess the hidden word in 6 tries. The color of the tiles will change to give you clues.

A
Green: Correct The letter is in the word and in the correct spot.
B
Yellow: Other Position The letter is in the word, but in another position.
C
Red: Not Included The letter is not in the hidden word.
💡 Progressive hints: You will get a hint on the 5th attempt and the full description at the end.
